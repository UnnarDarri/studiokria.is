**Raddir sem heyrast ekki** (Voices That Aren't Heard) was filmed over 18 months with a predominantly Deaf crew. The film uses visual storytelling techniques that center Deaf experience — minimizing voiceover, maximizing visual rhythm, and treating Icelandic Sign Language as the primary language of the film. Independent documentary 2026.
