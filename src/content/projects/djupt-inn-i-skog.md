**Djúpt inn í skóg** (Deep in the Forest) draws on Icelandic folklore about the hidden people and the ancient spirits that dwell in the old-growth forests. Shot on 16mm in Hallormsstaðaskógur, Iceland's largest forest, the film uses the natural environment as both setting and character.
