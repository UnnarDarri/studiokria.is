**Rólegur Gaur** (Calm Guy) was inspired by the Icelandic concept of "þetta reddast" (it'll work out) taken to its logical extreme. Shot in a single location over 6 days, the film uses static compositions and long takes to create comedic tension through stillness. School print film for Borgarholtsskóla, March 2026.
