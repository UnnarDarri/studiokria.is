**COULROPHOBIA** is a psychological horror short film that explores the primal fear of clowns. What begins as an innocent children's birthday party spirals into terror when the hired entertainer refuses to leave. The film uses practical effects and 35mm cinematography to create a visceral, unsettling atmosphere that lingers long after the credits roll.
